# Evidence publication notes

This repository publishes an empirical study and inspectable evidence. Downstream transmission and privilege escalation are conditional risk scenarios. Actual observations, hypotheses and unperformed checks are separated in the report.

The original native receipts are retained separately. Public projections omit operational host metadata and do not rewrite those receipts. The verifier reads local files without networking. Upstream model weights and third-party runtime binaries are not redistributed. Source documents are linked at fixed revisions. Original English/Russian experimental text is preserved unchanged.
